HOW TO PLAY

3-minute games, 1-minute sudden-death overtime, then a shootout. Every win earns 30 trophies, and trophies unlock tougher arenas. Your first win each day earns a card pack: gold, player cards for upgrades, or a new legend.

  • FACEOFF: tap any button the instant the puck drops. Too early and you lose it. Aim the pad to choose where you draw it.
  • PAD skate. You always control the skater with the gold ring. On a computer: WASD or arrows, Space shoots, Shift dekes, E passes, Esc pauses.
  • SHOOT hold to wind up, let go to fire. Push the pad left or right to pick a corner. Push it toward the net as you let go to go top shelf.
  • BREAKAWAY: get past every defender and the goalie comes out to challenge. Deke him and finish.
  • PASS goes to the open teammate, or the one you're pointing at. Teammates show their job above them; gold OPEN! means they're in a scoring spot with a clean lane.
  • DEKE side-steps and drags the puck across. Point the pad left or right to choose the side. Pokes and checks can't touch you mid-deke.
  • YOUR GOALIE: when your goalie gets the puck, YOU control him. Skate him around his crease (he can't leave it), then SHOOT or PASS it up. Hold it too long with someone on you and he covers it for a whistle.
  • \u{1F988} DAMIEN SHARK (ULTRA LEGEND): when you get him you pick his position for good: LW, RW, C, LD, RD or G. Centre = FACEOFF KING + perfect-passing super. Wingers = off-wing one-timers + a one-timer bomb super. Defence = SMELLS BLOOD + FEEDING FRENZY. Goalie = JAWS (no rebounds). Find him in Legendary card packs.
  • NEW PLAYERS: when a card pack brings someone new, tap MEET YOUR NEW PLAYER. Tap the mystery card 3 times to scout him, guess who it is (right = +100 gold), and watch him sign.
  • TROPHY ROADS: 3 on 3 and 5 on 5 each have their own trophies, arenas and road. A prize every 100 trophies.
  • 5 ON 5: switch between 3 ON 3 and 5 ON 5 above the PLAY button. In 5 on 5 each team dresses a C, LW, RW, LD and RD plus a goalie. Your deck plays C, a wing and D; two depth players fill the other wing and D. Friend games are always 3 on 3.
  • PULL THE GOALIE (🥅 button at the top, or G on a computer): your goalie goes to the bench and an extra attacker jumps on, 4 skaters against 3, but your net is EMPTY. Tap it again to put him back. He comes back on his own after any goal. The CPU pulls theirs too when they're down late.
  • SUPERS (Arena 3+): only LEGENDS have supers, each based on a real NHL star's signature move (like Halvorsen's THE OFFICE, inspired by Ovechkin, or Okafor's OPEN-ICE HIT, which sends someone off the ice). Tap SUPERS on the home screen to see what every one does. Uncommon and regular players don't have one. The purple SUPER button fills up as you play (faster when you shoot or land hits). When it says READY, tap it (C on a computer). The CPU always plays the same kind of deck as you (a legend for each of your legends, an uncommon for each uncommon) but with different players, and from Arena 3 up their legends use supers too.
  • BACKHAND: tap SHOOT right after a deke, or while skating away from the net, for an instant shot the goalie reads late.
  • On defence: POKE reaches out with the stick, CHECK lunges for a body hit, SWITCH changes skaters.
  • ICING: clear the puck from your half past their goal line and the faceoff comes back to your zone. OFFSIDE: the puck must cross the blue line before you do.
  • EMOTES: tap the speech bubble on the rink's edge (or T, then 1-9). You start with 3 and find more in Epic, Mythic and Legendary card packs.
  • SHOOTOUT: three shooters each, then sudden death. One chance per attempt, no rebounds.
SUPERS
Unlock in Arena 3

Shot supers (🎯 The Office, 💣 One-Timer Bomb, ☄️ 108 MPH Slapper) work on your next shot: tap SUPER and it says NEXT SHOT = THE OFFICE, then shoot like normal. Or hold SUPER, drag to aim (up = top shelf) and let go to fire right away. On a computer: tap C, or hold C and aim with A/D. Only legends have supers, and each one is based on a real NHL player's signature move. Uncommon and regular players don't get one. The purple SUPER button fills up as you play (faster when you shoot or hit). When it says READY, tap it (C on a computer). The super belongs to whoever you're controlling.
